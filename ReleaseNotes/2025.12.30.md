-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Known issues
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Memory leaks when playing videos in MPV, Firefox, or Chromium.  
2. Under heavy system load, MPV may experience a black screen during playback.  
3. In the sleep-wake scenario, the NPU may experience a work timeout.  
4. When using some power adapters, the first reset after updating the bootloader may get stuck.
